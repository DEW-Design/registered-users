# 2026-11-18 - The workspace panel: add, then the areas, with editing inside each area's own card

- **Nov 18 2026: Changed in the lab (`app/proto/expand-and-draw/areas-panel.tsx` new, `workspace-hybrid.tsx`, `workspace-split.tsx`, `map-drawing/ui.tsx`). Nothing in the product.** The designer, on the expanded workspace: "this panel needs to be made nicer. There's add on the top, existing points, then edit sits within existing points, no?" The panel's order was Add, then a separate "Polygon, 4 points" editor, then the Areas list, so the numbers being edited were cut off from the area they belong to.
  - **Now, in the order the job goes.** (1) *Add an area* (a heading, then the design system's tabs: Place, Coordinates, File; Split adds Draw). (2) *Areas* (a heading with the product's `CountBadge` once there is something to count, "Area" for a one-area project) and under it one card per area. (3) *Editing is inside the card:* the open card shows the area's name, its kind icon, its facts in a line ("4 points, 1,219 km²", "6.8 km radius, 145 km²"), the bin, and below a rule its editable coordinates (the table, or centre and radius for a circle) with one line about nudging; there is no separate editor above or below the list (checked: 0 "Selected area" sections, the editor inside the list's `li`). One card is open at a time: the one being edited (the area whose point was touched, or whose card was pressed, which also brings the shape into view). The open card has the brand border and halo that a card in edit mode has on the project page (4.8). (4) *A shape that has just been drawn is the first card,* "New polygon" with a "To check" badge, open, dashed when it is not the one in view, with "Use it from the bar under the map, or draw it again."; the used areas under it are dimmed and cannot be opened until it is used or drawn again (checked: card 1 "New polygon", badge, open; cards 2 and 3 closed).
  - **A fault found while building it.** The coordinate fields were cut off in the narrower card ("-34.6000"): 9 characters at 16px in a column 104px wide. The panel is 24rem (it was 22rem) and the fields are 14px with tabular figures and 8px of side padding; checked with 5-decimal values (-35.12345, 138.54321): 8 fields, none clipped.
  - **Agent calls (each reversible: `areas-panel.tsx`, or the one grid width in `workspace-hybrid.tsx`).** The card's layout (a 32px icon tile, name above facts, bin at the right); one open at a time and no way to close the open one except opening another (the first area opens by itself); the shorter hint line, which drops "click a small dot on an edge to add a point" (the dots show on hover and add a point when pressed); "New polygon" and "To check"; the used cards are inert while a shape waits.
  - **Open, for the designer.** Whether an area should be nameable (the cards say Polygon 1, Circle 1, and a place's own name). Whether the open card may be closed too (an accordion that can close all, which would let the list be seen whole while the map is edited). Whether Add should fold away once there are areas, to give the cards the height (it stays open now). Whether the one-line hint should say that the dots on an edge add a point.
