# 2026-11-18 - Transect starts with sample site dimensions and no IBRA, dimensions field fixed, a Save shows what it changed

- **Nov 18 2026: Transect starts with sample site dimensions and no IBRA, the dimensions field fixed, and a Save shows what it changed.**
  - **Dimensions field.** `DimensionsControl` handed back "Not provided" until both numbers were in, so typing the first number cleared the box and the field could never be filled. It now keeps a half-typed pair itself and hands back a value once both are in (`field-controls.tsx`).
  - **Transect defaults (designer, 18 Nov 2026).** A Transect opens with Sample site dimensions on the form (an empty concept is otherwise left out until added) and the IBRA region and subregion off. It holds for every Transect, the saved ones too: `KIND_DEFAULTS` in `field-visibility-store.ts`, read by `useHiddenFields(id, record)`. A person's own choice stays: ticking the IBRA concepts is remembered per record (`shown`), and unticking the dimensions is kept. A Quadrat is not changed (not asked). The Pre-fill sample content switch still shows every concept, as it is the fullest branch (3.14).
  - **Save shows what changed (designer, 18 Nov 2026).** `changedFieldKeys` compares the record before and after a save as the view reads it; `publishSavedChanges` hands the keys to whichever view is drawn next (the details panel drops its editor on save, so the editor cannot do it); `useShowSavedChanges` opens the cards, scrolls to the first field and holds the tint (`flashElement` `hold`, about 5 s). The project page's cards tint the saved card (`flashSavedCard`). Added to 4.8 item 5.
  - **Verified in a browser:** an existing Transect's view and edit show the dimensions, no IBRA; a new Transect with the sample switch off shows 4 of 7 location concepts, no IBRA; length typed alone is kept; a saved dimension tints its row (rgb 220, 236, 239); a saved project card tints. Not measured: touch, other roles, the full-screen view's highlight, Quadrat.
  - **Open:** an empty Sample site dimensions on a saved Transect is left out of the view as every empty concept is (2.3); whether the view should show it as "Not provided" is the designer's. The project page highlights the card, not the field (its fields have no ids). Whether Quadrat, which follows Transect in the business sheet, takes the same defaults.
