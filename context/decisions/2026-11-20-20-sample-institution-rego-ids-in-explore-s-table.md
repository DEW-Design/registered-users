# 2026-11-20 - Sample institution rego ids in Explore's table: SAM123 and AD for flora

- **Nov 20 2026: the Institution rego # column in Explore's table has sample ids.** The designer: "update the table-view on the explore tab, with a few rego ids - SAM123, etc. currently incorrect." The column had register words ("Birds", "Mammals") or nothing (`2026-11-20-19` removed the derived words once the field became free text).
  - **Changed.** `sampleRego` (`survey-data.ts`) makes a sample id: SAM for fauna (the South Australian Museum), AD for flora (the State Herbarium's acronym), and a number, 100 to 999, made from the record's own id so a record has the same id on every visit (SAM607, AD121). Every species occurrence gets one unless it holds its own: Explore's seeded records (`project-seed.ts`, by species group), and the hand-written Adelaide Hills records (`occurrence()`, by taxonomic type). The one hand-written voucher, the Southern Brown Bandicoot's, is now "SAM123", the designer's own example, in place of the register word "Mammals". `OccurrenceFields.rego` carries it for a record without a voucher, on both an Individual's and a Population's Voucher section. The field stays free text, and "Pre-fill sample content" still leaves it empty.
  - **Sample data (0.3).** These are invented demo identifiers, requested by the designer, not catalogue numbers: marked as sample in `sampleRego`'s comment. This is the one place an invented identifier is allowed, by the designer's instruction for this table, and it supersedes the "no invented number" line of `-19`.
  - **Verified live** (BioData Admin, Explore, Map and table): all 31 rows show a rego, none empty; fauna SAM108 to SAM607, flora AD120 to AD608, the bandicoot SAM123; zero console errors. `tsc` and `check:contracts` clean.
  - **Open.** Whether flora should read AD or another prefix, and whether the sample ids should be shorter or follow the voucher numbers, are for the designer. Not committed.
