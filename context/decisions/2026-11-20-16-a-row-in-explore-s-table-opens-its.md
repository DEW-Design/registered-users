# 2026-11-20 - A row in Explore's table opens its species card, and an edge-case pass over the split view

- **Nov 20 2026: pressing a row of the Map and table view opens that species' card and moves the map to its dot, as a dot does.** The designer, with a screenshot: "if i click on a species on the table should also bring up the species card, no? in the table view", and "can we run through any edge cases that may break the design?"
  - **Built.** `DataReport` takes opt-in `onRowAction` (a click, or Enter on a focused row; rows are pointers), `rowActionable` (a row with no record is not pressable) and `activeRowId` (the row whose card is open, in the brand tint, so the table says which row the card is for). Explore passes them: the row's record is the Explore occurrence its dot belongs to (`selectRecord`, so the map centres on it). The row collection is told about `activeRowId` (`dependencies`), or react-aria keeps the old render and the tint never moves. The compact card (`-15`) is the card shown.
  - **A species picked in the table is on the map.** With nothing searched the map has no dots, so the card had none to belong to (and `peekVisible` needs a dot): `mapMarkers` now adds the picked species while the table is open, kept only while the search still lists its row (the same words the table's search matches). The table's `tableRows` moved above `mapMarkers` for it.
  - **Edge cases run** (Playwright, registered user unless named; zero console errors throughout): no search, click a row: card, one dot, one tinted row. A second row swaps the card; Escape closes it and clears the tint. A row with no Explore record (the Adelaide Hills project's own survey records, 2 rows): no pointer, a click does nothing. Keyboard: a focused row opens its card on Enter. The table's full screen: no card (there is no map), exiting keeps working. Switching to the map-only view with a card open: the full card and its dot stay. A search that leaves the picked row out: the card closes (it did not, until the derived check above; found by this pass). Public user: 32 rows, a click opens the card. Window sizes 1716 by 1034, 1440 by 900, 1280 by 720, 1024 by 768, 768 by 900: the card never overlaps the zoom buttons (2.8), no sideways page scroll; a species with the restricted-location note (the tallest card) is 289px at 1716 by 1034, and scrolls with its fade at 1440 by 900 (231 of 287), 1280 by 720 (141) and 1024 by 768 (146). `tsc`, `eslint`, `check:contracts` clean.
  - **Found and not fixed, for the designer (0.4).** (1) A phone (390 by 844) cannot use the split view: the map is 243px tall, the search card alone is 170px, so it is squashed to 72px with its field and the Map | table toggle cut off (before this change too), the species card is 2px, and the toggle is the way back to the map. A floating layout of search card, card, key and zoom buttons has no room in 243px; recommended: below about 640px the table view is the table at full height (the table's full screen, which has its own way back), with no split. (2) In a short window (720 to 768px) the restricted species card shows only its photo, name and project, the rest behind the scroll and fade; a shorter restricted note, or the facts before the note, would help, but both are copy and order decisions. (3) The Filter menu hiding the picked row leaves the card open (the pick was the person's own). (4) Rows with no record (2 of 32) have no card; their data lives only in the project's survey records, with no dot.
