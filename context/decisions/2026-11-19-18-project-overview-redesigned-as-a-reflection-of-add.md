# 2026-11-19 - Project Overview redesigned as a reflection of Add Project, three whole-page options in a lab

- **Nov 19 2026: Project Overview redesigned as a reflection of Add Project, three whole-page options in a lab.**
  **The designer's brief.** After the people-only lab (`2026-11-19-17`): "I wanted a redesign of the whole page. Visit the project creation flow. The overview tab is a reflection of what the user provides in the project creation." This continues `-17`, which is left as it was written: its three people treatments are reused here as the people block.
  **What the flow collects, and what the page made of it (found by walking Add Project with sample content, creating the project, and opening its Overview).** The flow has three steps, Project Identification, Data Collection and Methodology, Privacy and Restrictions, and asks: project name, full title, abstract, dates; one or more data owners each with officers (role, one primary contact); geographic extent; focus areas (with an Other); a methodology, and for a Systematic or Analysis project a list of methodologies each with its own "variation, limitation or bias" note; permits and URI/DOI; whether restrictions apply and which of five kinds. The Overview reads this as nine cards of label and value rows in other words ("Short title", "Focus, species and method", "Collection method"). Against a project the flow made: the methodologies are joined into one comma string ("Bird survey, 500 metre area search, Bird survey, 20 minutes, 2 hectare, Dusk roost count...") that cannot be read because the names hold commas; "None required / Not provided / Not provided" is three rows; five restrictions are five tall cards, the species one listing every concept and justification; the abstract is squeezed into a 110px column beside a 11rem label; the people are listed twice (Published by and Project managers); "Targeted species" is shown though the flow no longer asks it.
  **Principles at stake (0.10).** Tell the truth (4): the page says what was given, in the words it was asked in, in the form it was given (a list is a list; the same summaries the Review step writes, 2.15). Say less (1) and One thing first (2): nine cards at one weight, and a row for every field. Reveal in steps (6): the answer first, its detail a click away. Design every state (7): everything given, one restriction, none; two owners, twelve people, none current. Where they pull apart: Say less against Make it obvious. Folding is less to read and one more click to find a thing.
  **What was built (a lab only, `app/proto/project-overview/`; nothing in the product changed).** `content.ts` (the project read in the flow's words: its step titles, its section and field names, `restrictionSummaryFor`, methodologies as a list, empty answers left out), `blocks.tsx` (one block per kind of answer: prose, people, a place, chips, a list with a note each as the basic table, facts, a restriction row that opens), `overview.tsx` (the three options), `people.ts` and `people-ui.tsx` (from `-17`, with a Current or Not active status held in the lab), `page.tsx` (the lab; its two tools are Project given and People shown). The project is not hand-written: it is Add Project's own sample (`sampleProjectForm`, saved by `toSavedForm`), narrowed to "Everything given", "A typical project" (one embargo, one methodology, a permit and a number) and "Only what is required" (no restrictions). (1) **Briefing:** one column, the three chapters one under another, everything in view; prose as prose, owners as cards, the extent beside focus areas and permits, methodologies as a two-column table (the methodology, then its note), restrictions as rows. (2) **Map-led:** the extent map stays in view (sticky) beside the chapters. (3) **Folded:** each chapter says its answer while closed (the extent, the focus areas, the methodology; the kinds of restriction) and opens to the whole; Identification starts open, its people as one line per owner and a dialog.
  **Changes every option makes to the page, for the designer to confirm one by one (0.4, 4.7).** The chapters and blocks are named as Add Project names them, not as the page does now. "Overview" and the line under each section heading are not drawn (2.12); "Short title" is not a row (4.3); the full title shows only where it differs. No second Project managers list: a project manager is a person whose role is Project Manager (16 Oct 2026). A methodology is a list with its note, not one string. "Targeted species" is not shown. An answer not given is left out and said once at the foot of the chapter ("Not provided: URI / DOI"); the coordinates table is behind "Show coordinates". Each restriction is a row: its name as Add Project's column 2 has it, the line its Review writes, and what was entered on opening. Status, Current or Not active, on every person. New words: the three chapter titles in the flow's title case, "Data owner(s)", "Full title", "Other project officers", "Primary contact", "Show coordinates", "Show N not active", "View all", "N kinds of restriction apply".
  **Decided by the agent, to confirm.** The numbered step markers of the flow are not drawn (a number tells a reader nothing on a page that is not a form). The restriction summaries are the flow's own strings, so a date in one reads 19/11/2027 while the header reads 30 June 2029; they were not re-worded (2.15). Restricted locations were tried on the map with the extent: the shared map draws every shape alike, so the extent and the restricted areas cannot be told apart. Telling them apart is a change to the map component (1.10, the designer's override), so it was not made and the restricted locations are named in their row only.
  **Research (reported to the designer, not a source of styling, 2.6).** As `-17`: GOV.UK's DfE design history moved contacts to a tab to keep the about page short; Total Synergy's overview has a People panel and a clickable status; Ghostwriter marks one contact primary; Primer limits an avatar stack to two to four and says to avoid it where there is room; hover cards are for preview detail and need a keyboard and touch way in; a status is read from text, not an indicator alone; Notion pins about four properties under the title; Linear has a summary, a longer description and a details sidebar. Two pages could not be read directly (DfE contacts 503, Linear overview 404): those two rest on search summaries.
  **Open.** (1) Which option, or a mix (a folded Map-led is possible). (2) Whether Project managers stays its own card (CONTRACTS 4.7 says it has one) or is the role on a person. (3) Where status is set (a switch in the officer's form, or a "left on" date) and whether a new officer is current by default. (4) Whether "Targeted species" still belongs on the page, and whether the Overview should keep showing it for projects that hold it. (5) The chapter names in title case against the page's sentence case ("Data collection and methodology"). (6) Whether the restriction summaries should show their dates as the page does. (7) Whether the restricted locations should be drawn on the map, which needs the map to style them apart. (8) The page's "On this page" column lists the cards; it is not drawn in the options and would follow the chosen chapters. (9) The registration's own Review step and the page could then share one summary function.
