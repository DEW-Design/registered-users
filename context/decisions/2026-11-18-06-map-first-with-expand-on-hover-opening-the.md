# 2026-11-18 - Map first, with Expand on hover opening the Hybrid workspace

- **Nov 18 2026: Decided, in direction. Exploring the details at proto.** The designer, on the expanded workspace ("The full screen experience is a bit unthought") and a screenshot of "Check it first": "What I want is the check it first option - show map by default, on hover, show the expand button. Then have the hybrid option on expand." So the form shows the map (the steps of "check it first": choose a shape, draw it, check it, use it) and an Expand button on hover; Expand opens the Hybrid workspace. This refines `2026-11-18-04` (Expand and draw replaces the four tabs): the form no longer holds a summary card, it holds the map. Route: `/proto/expand-and-draw`, variant 2, "Map first" (`map-first.tsx`); the earlier summary-first versions stay as variants 3 and 4 for comparison (nothing under `/proto` is deleted before a pull request).
  - **Decided (the designer's words).** (1) The map is shown by default in the form, with the steps of "check it first". (2) Expand appears on hover. (3) The expanded view is the Hybrid workspace.
  - **What "unthought" was, as found (and what was done about it).** The expanded screen was a separate place: it opened on its own view of the map, its own header, and a shape part-drawn or waiting to be checked did not come with you. Now it is the same map continued: (a) the map's view is held (`view` in `drawing.ts`), so the expanded map opens at the same centre and zoom and the form's map comes back at the view the expanded one left (checked: the scale read 1 km inline, 1 km expanded, 1 km after); (b) a shape waiting to be checked comes with you and is checked in the workspace's panel with its coordinates, with "Use this area" and "Redraw" (a shape that is drawn in the workspace is checked the same way); (c) expanding in the middle of a drawing keeps it (2 points inline, expanded, a third click gives "3 points"); (d) the workspace's header says what is there ("A new polygon to check", "1 area"), and its "Use" uses a shape still waiting; (e) exact coordinates are edited in the workspace, so the form's check step is a look at the whole shape under the map (a bar below it, not a sheet over it, which hid the shape): "Edit coordinates" is a way into Expand.
  - **Expand, on hover.** Hidden until the pointer is over the map or focus is in it (an opacity change of 150ms, nothing else moves); always shown where there is no hover (checked with a touch device: opacity 1, `hover: none`), so it is not only for a mouse. It sits at the top right, since the "go to a place" box is at the top left while choosing.
  - **A fault found and fixed while checking, in this and in `2026-11-18-01`'s "Check it first".** The step header grew from one line to two as a shape was drawn, which pushed the map down by 28px under the pointer, so the next click missed its point. The header keeps a height that holds two lines and does not wrap its buttons under the text (`min-h-[4.75rem]`, the buttons do not shrink), and the map measured at the same place (226px) through all the clicks. On a phone the text may still run to three lines and move the map; not fixed.
  - **Agent calls (each reversible by deleting `map-first.tsx`, or the named place).** The wording of the check line ("Is this the area?", "Looks right? Use it, or draw it again. Exact coordinates are in Expand."); the button's name, "Edit coordinates", for the way into Expand from the check step; a map that is open elsewhere is a box of the same size while the workspace is open (one live map at a time); a shape waiting to be checked is framed on the map when it is finished; the Expand button's place and its icon (`Maximize02`, as in the product's "Full screen").
  - **Open, for the designer.** Whether Expand should also be where the other three ways of adding an area (place, coordinates, file) are found, since the form's map only draws; or whether they should be in the form too (an "add another way" row under the map). Whether a keyboard user should reach Expand without the hover (it is reached by Tab, and appears on focus, but is not announced as the way to precision). The one-or-several-areas, where-it-opens and Cancel questions of `2026-11-18-05` still stand. The inline map is 440px high, as the form's was; whether it should be taller now that precision is in Expand.
  - **Verified (18 Nov 2026, `http://localhost:3000/proto/expand-and-draw?v=2`, Chromium headless, mouse and keyboard):** zero console errors. The map is there at once; Expand's opacity is 0 until the pointer is over the map, then 1. A polygon drawn inline reads "Polygon, 4 points, 38 km². Is this the area?" with the bar below the map. "Edit coordinates" opened the workspace at the same scale (1 km both), with the coordinates in the panel and "Use this area" and "Redraw"; typing a latitude changed the area (38 to 40 km²); "Use this area" in the panel and then in the header closed it with the area in the form's list at the same scale. Expanding after two points kept them. On a touch device (390px, `hover: none`) Expand shows without hover, the page does not overflow, and the workspace's map is 364 by 373px. `npx tsc --noEmit` and `npx eslint app/proto` pass.
  - **Not checked.** Dragging and the edge dots inside the workspace after expanding from a check; focus returning to Expand when the workspace closes; a screen reader; the three-line header on a phone; the inline map with "One area" and "Opens on the project's place"; whether the opacity change under reduced motion needs removing (it is a fade, not a movement).
