# 2026-11-18 - Making a drawn point exact at proto, typed values, nudging, zoom and edge points

- **Nov 18 2026: Exploring. Nothing is decided.** The designer, on `2026-11-18-01`: "if I wanted to edit / make a point precise once I've drawn them in? What then?" Answered in the lab, not in the product (`/proto/map-drawing`).
  - **What the three variants did before this, found by asking the question of each.** *Panel:* typed numbers and dragging both worked on a selected shape. *On the map:* the card showed the coordinates as read-only text, so a drawn point could be dragged but never typed. *Check it first:* the table was editable only until "Use this area"; a shape that had been used could not be opened again. None had a way to place a point by eye closely, to add a point to a shape, or to see the numbers while dragging.
  - **What is there now, in all three.** (1) *Typed values.* Every point's latitude and longitude, and a circle's centre and radius, are fields; a half-typed value is not an error, one outside South Australia is flagged on the field and put back when you leave it. (2) *Nudge.* Up and down change the focused number by 0.00001 degrees (about a metre), ten times that with Shift; a circle's radius moves by 0.1 km. (3) *Zoom to a point.* A button on each row flies the map to that point at street level (the scale went from 3 km to 100 m in the check), so a point can be dragged precisely by eye; a circle has "Zoom to the centre". (4) *A live readout while dragging.* The pointer's coordinates follow the handle (shown in the Panel's and Check it first's footer and the On the map corner chip). (5) *Add a point on an edge.* A small dot sits on the middle of every edge of a selected polygon; pressing it adds a point there that can be dragged (4 to 5 in the check). Removing a point is the bin on its row (a polygon keeps three). (6) *Edit after use* in Check it first: each used area has Edit, which takes it back to the checking sheet, and "Use this area" puts it back.
  - **Agent calls (each reversible by deleting `app/proto/map-drawing`, or the one named place).** The nudge size and the Shift multiple (`step` in `CoordInput`, `ui.tsx`); five decimal places as the precision shown (about a metre); a point is added at the middle of an edge, not where the edge was pressed; On the map's card keeps the coordinates collapsed until "Edit coordinates" (`editing` in `variant-hud.tsx`), because open they cover about half the map; the wording of the one hint line (`PRECISION_HINT`, `ui.tsx`).
  - **Found and fixed while checking (engine, `draw-map.tsx`).** (a) Pressing a zoom button counted as a click on the map, so it deselected the shape (and mid-draw it would have added a point); the zoom buttons now stop the click reaching the map. (b) With the coordinates open, On the map's tall card covered the zoom buttons; its height is now capped so it clears them.
  - **What it costs.** On the map and Panel now carry a table of points, which at 20 or more points is long; it scrolls inside the card, and the nudge and zoom are per row, so it is slow for a shape with many points. Adding a point on an edge only halves the edge; a very short edge is hard to hit. Precision to a metre is the display, not the survey's: the lab does not know the datum (the product asks for WGS84).
  - **Open, for the designer.** How exact a point has to be (a metre, a centimetre, to a survey grid), and in which datum; whether a point may be placed by typing an address or a place name (the "go to" box only moves the map); whether the numbers should be shown in degrees and decimals only, or also in degrees, minutes and seconds; whether a long list of points should be pasted, not edited a row at a time (`/proto/geographic-extent`'s Coordinates grid does this).
  - **Verified (18 Nov 2026, `http://localhost:3000/proto/map-drawing`, Chromium headless, mouse and keyboard):** zero console errors. *On the map:* point 1's latitude typed as -34.97000, Up twice gave -34.96998, Shift and Down gave -34.97008; typing -90 flagged the field (`aria-invalid`) and it went back to -34.97008 on leaving; Zoom to point 2 changed the scale from 3 km to 100 m; the dot on an edge took the polygon from 4 to 5 points; the pointer readout showed coordinates while a handle was dragged. *Check it first:* a used circle's Edit reopened the sheet (radius 2.6), 5 was typed, and "Use this area" gave "Circle 1, 5 km". `npx tsc --noEmit` and `npx eslint app/proto` pass.
  - **Not checked.** The nudge and the edge dots by touch; a screen reader on the table; the circle's radius by arrow keys in the Panel; a shape of 40 or more points; the type and spacing of the longer card against 2.9 by `getComputedStyle`. Not committed.
