# 2026-11-18 - Dragging the points of a drawn shape, smoother at proto

- **Nov 18 2026: Changed in the lab (the drawing engine, `app/proto/map-drawing/draw-map.tsx`). Nothing in the product.** The designer, on the "Map first" check step: "edits are great! can we make sure editing (dragging the points after they're drawn) is smoother?" Worked through `/emil-interaction-details`: each change below names the question of its walk that found it, what it did before, what it does now, with the number that decides it.
  - **The finding that mattered most (Tracking, "the element is the pointer").** Dragging a point barely worked. Measured on the live page with a real mouse and the CPU slowed 4 times: the pointer moved 184px and the handle moved **3px**. Cause: every update built a new icon object for the handle, and Leaflet replaces a marker's element when its icon changes, so the handle held by the pointer was swapped out from under it a few pixels in. Icons are now made once and reused (`iconCache`). After: the handle moved 182px of 184px, 3px behind the pointer, with no frame over 17ms.
  - **Click on a handle deselected the shape (Intent, "a press is not a drag, and it is not a press on the map").** A press on a point that did not move it counted as a press on the map, which deselected the shape and hid every handle; Leaflet stops a click at a marker only if the marker listens for it, and the handles did not. Before: 4 handles, then 0 after a plain click. After: 4, then 4. The same cause meant a tiny wobble while pressing lost the shape.
  - **Drag start (Intent, "can it fire by accident").** The point is picked up after the pointer has moved 2px with a mouse and 10px with a finger (Leaflet's `clickTolerance` on the handle's draggable, which was 3 for both). A 1px wobble: the point stayed at (1070, 304), the shape stayed selected.
  - **Writers (Tracking).** While a point is held, React does not move it: the marker is given the spot it was picked up from, so Leaflet's own drag is the only writer and nothing pulls it back a frame. The shape and the numbers follow once a frame with the latest position (`requestAnimationFrame`), not once for every pointer event, and the last position is applied on release. Live numbers while dragging a polygon's point: Area 33 to 41 km²; a circle's radius handle: 3.7 km to 6.2 km, 43 to 121 km² (the pointer 80px, the handle 80px).
  - **A held handle shows it is held (Intent, "show the press").** It grows to 1.3 times over 120ms (`ease-out`) and the cursor stays "grabbing" wherever the pointer goes until release (checked: the dot's transform `matrix(1.3, …)` and `body` cursor `grabbing` while held, both cleared after). Only the dot grows; the 28px target does not move under the pointer.
  - **Limits (Limits, "dragging toward the edge scrolls").** A held point near the map's edge pans the map after it (`autoPan`, 64px from the edge, speed 12). Dragging a point of a 438px map toward its corner moved the map pane from (-54, 44) to (-839, 462). Not a tension curve: nothing here has a hard end.
  - **Changing their mind (Patience, "Escape cancels").** Escape while a point is held puts it back where it was picked up, with nothing committed (the area went from 41 back to 33 km²). If the map panned on the way (autoPan), it stays where it panned to.
  - **Keys (Repetition).** A focused point (each is a tab stop, with a name: "Point 2. Drag it, or use the arrow keys.") moves a pixel with an arrow key and ten with Shift, whatever the zoom, and the map does not pan (3 presses of Right: 3px; Shift+Down: 10px).
  - **Touch (Intent, 44px).** On a coarse pointer (`pointer: coarse`) the handle's target is 44px (the dot is still 12px), 28px with a mouse; the edge dots follow. Checked as 44, 44, 44, 44 on a 390px touch device.
  - **Checked and left alone.** The edge dots (add a point) still add one (4 to 5); typing, nudging in the table, zoom to a point, Expand with the same view, Use and Cancel all still pass their earlier checks; the pointer readout still shows the point's coordinates while it is dragged. No inertia: a point you let go of stays where it is (there is nothing to throw). No tension at the edge of South Australia: a point dragged outside is flagged on its row in the table, not stopped.
  - **Agent calls (each reversible in `draw-map.tsx`).** 1.3 times for the held dot; 120ms; autoPan padding 64px and speed 12; the 2px and 10px thresholds; the arrow keys' 1px and 10px; one position write per frame.
  - **Needs a real device.** The 10px finger threshold and the 44px targets by touch (only the size was checked, with an emulated touch device); how autoPan feels at its speed on a laptop trackpad; whether a throw should carry a point at all; whether Escape should also put the map back. The 4 times CPU slowdown is an emulation of a slow machine, not a measurement of one.
  - **Not checked.** Dragging a point of a polygon with 40 or more points; two fingers on one handle; the inline map and the expanded workspace at once (only one map is live at a time); a screen reader on the point names.
  - **Touches:** contracts 1.10 (the behaviour is built in the lab and moves to `components/**` with the designer's override if chosen), 2.16, 5.4; `2026-11-18-02`, `-06`.
