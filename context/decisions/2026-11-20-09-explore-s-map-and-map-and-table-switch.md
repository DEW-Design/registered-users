# 2026-11-20 - Explore's Map and Map and table switch is a pair of toggles beside the search field

- **Nov 20 2026: Changed in the product (`app/pages/observations/observations-search.tsx`, `components/application/tabs/tabs.tsx`).** The designer: "Adding map | table as toggles next to the search bar might be better UX." The switch was a pill in the map's bottom-left corner (`2026-11-19-12`); it is now two toggles at the right of the search field, inside the Explore card, 40px high like the field. The bottom-left corner is left to the species key.
  - **Icon only, with the name as its tooltip.** The card is 440px wide and the search field needs about 225px for its icon and placeholder, so two words do not fit beside it (about 170px for "Map | Table" left the field about 228px, truncating "Search a species or keyword"). The toggles are the map icon and the table icon, 44px wide each, 84px for the pair, leaving the field 314px. Each is named for a screen reader ("Map only", "Map and table") and shows that name as its tooltip (the native one, a box as big as the tab's padding). "Map and table" keeps its full name because "Table" alone would suggest the map goes away; the table view is the map and the table. If you want the words, "Map" and "Table" fit only at the cost of the field.
  - **`Tab` gains an opt-in `iconOnly`** (`components/application/tabs/tabs.tsx`): the label becomes the accessible name and the tooltip and is not drawn; the default is unchanged, so no other tab changes (1.6). It is used only here.
  - **Where it is and what it does.** In the card's search row, in both views (the card stays in "Map and table"), selected state as the design system's `button-border` tabs draw it. Pressing "Map and table" shows the table under the map; pressing "Map only" returns. What is typed stays, and the results do not change.
  - **Measured (20 Nov 2026, Chromium headless, 1600 by 950).** The field and the toggles share a top (217px) and a height (40px); the pair is 100px wide, each toggle 44 by 40; the field 298px (it was 406). The names and tooltips: "Map only" and "Map and table". "Map and table": the table section appears, the card and the typed "kangaroo" stay; "Map only": the table goes, "Map only" is selected. There is no switch left in the map's corner. Screenshot of the split looked at. Zero console errors; `tsc`, `eslint --max-warnings=0` on `app/pages` and the tabs, and `check:contracts` clean.
  - **Polish pass (same day, `/emil-ui-polish`, after the designer's screenshot of the pair).** The selected toggle's raised white tile was bigger than the group's grey well and spilled over its edge: the icon was a grandchild of the tab, so the tab's own icon size rule (16px) did not reach it and it drew at 24px, making each tab 44 by 40 inside a 40px group. Fixed in `Tab iconOnly` (the icon is 16px, the tab is the icon's box and nothing more): each tab is 36 by 32 inset 4px in the 84 by 40 group, as the design system's other `button-border` tabs sit in theirs, and the radii are concentric (the group's 10px less the 4px inset is the tab's 6px). The group is now 84px wide (it was 100), the field 314px. The two touch, so the group caps the hit-area extension (`--hit-min`, 2.16) and their targets are as drawn (36 by 32). The icon takes the tab's text colour (selected a darker grey than unselected) since the tab's `data-icon` colour rules cannot reach it.
  - **Search row moved up (same day, a later note: "move search up").** The gap between the title ("Search the map") and the search row was 16px; it is 8px (the header's bottom padding 12px to 8px, the row's top padding 4px to 0), so the field and the toggles sit closer under the title and the card is 8px shorter. Measured: title bottom to field top 8px, the field and the toggles share a top and a height (40px), the hint 6px under them; the same with a word typed. With the Add area form open the row keeps the 12px under the form. **Read as** closing that gap: the note's red lines marked the row's edges, not a destination. If "up" means into the title row (the field beside "Search the map" in place of the eyebrow), say so; that moves the title.
  - **Agent calls (reversible: `viewSwitch` in `observations-search.tsx`).** (1) Icon only and not words (above). (2) The native tooltip, not the design system's `Tooltip`: that one wraps a pressable of its own and a react-aria `Tab` is not one, so it cannot hold it. (3) The two sit in the search row and not in the card's header with New search and Add area.
  - **Not checked.** The hint under the field ("Search all of South Australia, or add an area.", which now sits under a narrower field); keyboard (arrow keys between the two, the focus ring); a narrow card (below 648px); the Add area form open above the row; the tooltip's look (a browser tooltip).
  - **Open.** Words instead of icons ("Map | Table") if the field's width can give. A tooltip in the design system's style (a component change).
