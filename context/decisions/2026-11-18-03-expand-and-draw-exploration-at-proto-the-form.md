# 2026-11-18 - Expand and draw exploration at proto, the form holds a summary and the drawing has the window

- **Nov 18 2026: Exploring. Nothing is decided.** The designer, with a screenshot of "Check it first" at the form's width: "What if we say expand and draw? Then it would make more sense? no?" The screenshot is the evidence: at 440px high the sheet of coordinates covers most of the map, so the shape just drawn is behind it (the polygon of 309,588 km² is not visible). Agreed, and explored, not built into the product: `/proto/expand-and-draw` (`app/proto/expand-and-draw/`), in the `/proto` index; round 2 of `2026-11-18-01` and `-02`. Keys 1 to 3 and the arrows switch.
  - **The idea, in one line.** The form holds a summary of the area and one button ("Expand and draw", "Expand and edit" once there is an area); the drawing, the numbers and the precision tools are in a workspace that takes the window, with "Cancel" (the areas go back as they were) and "Use these areas". The product's version 2 of Add Project already has an Expand button on its map (`ExpandableMap`, 9 Oct 2026); this makes the expanded view the place the work is done, and the form's map a picture.
  - **The form's part, shared by every variant (`shell.tsx`).** Empty: a dashed card, "No area yet", a line naming the four ways in, and the one button. With areas: a still map (the product's map, fitted to them, not pressable), "n areas" with their kinds and "about n km²" (overlaps count twice), Remove all, and Expand and edit. Opening to edit zooms to the areas that are there.
  - **The three variants.**
    1. *Current.* The product's picker with its own Expand (`GeoExtentPicker`, `expand="modal"`), imported live.
    2. *Split.* Axis: a notebook beside the map. The map takes the room; a panel (under the map on a narrow window) has four ways to add an area as tabs (Draw, Place, Coordinates, File; each adds to the same list, so they can be mixed), then the selected shape's editable coordinates with the precision tools of `-02`, then the list of every area. The shape and its numbers are on screen together.
    3. *On the map.* Axis: the map is the whole window. `/proto/map-drawing`'s "On the map" (tool column, one pill with the instruction and live numbers, the pointer, a card for the selected shape with its coordinates behind "Edit coordinates") given the room; the areas are chips under the map. Only drawing is on offer in it (no place, paste or file), which is the cost of that direction.
  - **Agent calls (each reversible by deleting `app/proto/expand-and-draw`, or the named place).** The three variants and their axes; Cancel restores the areas as they were when the workspace opened and the Escape key is not a way out of it (it cancels a drawing, as everywhere in the lab); "Use these areas" is disabled with no areas; the summary's "about n km²"; the tabs' labels, which are icons only on a phone; the Place choice adds a 15 km circle round the place, as the product's picker does, and a point from pasted coordinates gets 25 km, as the registration does.
  - **What it costs.** One more press to start (the map in the form is a picture, not a place to draw), and a second layer on the page (the product has one such dialog already). The summary's still map adds height to the form. *Split* needs the width of a window to be good (on a phone the map is 251px tall with the panel under it). *On the map* has no place, paste or file.
  - **Open, for the designer.** Which variant, or which parts. Whether "Expand and draw" replaces the four tabs of the form (so the form never holds a map to draw on) or sits beside them. Whether the workspace should open already zoomed to the project's place (it opens on the whole state until there is an area), and from what: the project's data owner, a typed place, none. Whether Cancel should ask when areas have been added. And the open items of `2026-11-17-11` and `2026-11-18-01` and `-02` still stand (several areas change the data; a lab is not removed from the static build in this repo, 5.4; precision and datum).
