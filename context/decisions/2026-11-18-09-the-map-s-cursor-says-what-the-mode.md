# 2026-11-18 - The map's cursor says what the mode is, and a shape waiting to be checked cannot be deselected

- **Nov 18 2026: Changed in the lab (`app/proto/map-drawing/draw-map.tsx`, `drawing.ts`). Nothing in the product.** The designer, with a screen recording of "Map first" (25.9s, 1444 by 1314 at about 58 frames a second): "between 20 and 25 seconds, the cursor sort of glitches, and it's a bit weird to know what the user is doing." The frames were read at one-second steps (the recording's own cursor is drawn in it).
  - **What the recording shows.** At 5s a six-point shape is waiting to be checked and none of its points is drawn; the cursor is an open hand. At 12s a polygon is being drawn (7 points, "Click the first point, double-click or press Enter to finish.") and the cursor is an open hand, the map's pan cursor, while a press places a point. At 14s to 19s the points are drawn and a point is dragged (Area 177,967 then 197,291 then 233,680 km²), with the cursor over an edge dot at 19s as an arrow with a green plus (`cursor: copy`). From 20s to 25s the points are gone again, the shape has not changed (233,680 km², 7 points, the same notch at its sixth point), and the cursor sits around that point as an open hand, once an arrow (22s): a hand that says "grab this" over a point that is not there to grab.
  - **Two causes, both mine.** (1) *The cursor described the element under it, not the mode.* The map's own pan cursor (an open hand) showed everywhere, so it said "drag the map" while drawing, a pointing hand on a shape that was not selected, a "copy" arrow with a green plus on the edge dot ("duplicate"?), and an arrow over a point it had just been a hand over. (2) *A shape waiting to be checked could be put down.* Pressing empty map deselected it, which hid its points and left the step still saying "Check the area you drew" with nothing to edit; the cursor still promised a grab. I could not see in the frames which press deselected it (at 19s the pointer is about 20px from an edge dot, which is a 24px target); the fix removes the way it could happen either way.
  - **Now.** (1) *The mode decides the cursor.* Drawing: a crosshair over the whole map, shapes included (checked: `crosshair` on empty map), except the point that closes the shape, a pointing hand (checked: `pointer`). A selected shape and its points: an open hand (checked: `grab` over the shape and over a point), and a point grows to 1.15 times under the pointer. A shape that is not selected: a pointing hand (a press selects it). The dot on an edge: a pointing hand, growing to 1.6 times under the pointer (a press adds a point there), no longer the "copy" cursor (measured transforms 1.15 and 1.6). Held, the cursor stays closed ("grabbing") wherever the pointer goes (`2026-11-18-08`). The growth is off under reduced motion. (2) *A shape waiting to be checked is always selected.* Pressing empty map or another shape does nothing to it; it is put away only by Use this area or Redraw. Checked: 4 points shown, press the empty map, 4 points shown, and a point still dragged (-60, +40 for a 60 by 40 move).
  - **Why it read as "weird to know what the user is doing".** The interface gave two answers at once: the step said "check, edit if needed", and the map offered nothing to edit while its cursor offered a grab. One thing should say what is possible: the points are always there while checking, and the cursor says "draw" or "grab" or "press" truthfully.
  - **Agent calls (each reversible in `draw-map.tsx`, the `CURSORS` block, or the one line in `drawing.ts`).** The scale on hover (1.15, 1.6); a pointing hand on the edge dot, not a crosshair or a plus cursor; a shape waiting to be checked cannot be deselected even by selecting another area (in the lab, with one pending shape at a time).
  - **Open, for the designer.** Whether the edge dot should be dragged to add a point in one move (press and drag creates the point and carries it, as map editors do) instead of press, then drag; that needs the dot to become the new point under the pointer, more than this change. Whether a shape that is not waiting (used, or in a list) should show its points on hover, so it is clear that pressing it selects it.
