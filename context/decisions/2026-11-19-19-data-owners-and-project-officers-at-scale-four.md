# 2026-11-19 - Data owners and project officers at scale: four ways in a lab, accordion kept

- **Nov 19 2026: Data owners and project officers at scale: four ways in a lab, accordion kept.**
  **The designer's brief.** With a screenshot of the Contact first owner cards and one of the page's accordion: "What else could we do to show the data owners and the project officers in each data ownership. We are expecting multiple data owners and a lot of project officers. Open to ideas of showing project officer on progressive disclosure as well. Also, I like how we had used accordion here for the page." Continues `2026-11-19-17` and `-18`, which stay as written.
  **Principles at stake (0.10).** Reveal in steps (6): the answer first, the many behind a click. One thing first (2): an owner's primary contact. Design every state (7): one owner and three officers, five owners and forty, no one current, none named, a role that sees no people. One system (3): the page's own accordion, embedded table, search and Filter, and the design system's popover. Where they pull apart: Say less against Make it obvious. A person behind a click is less to read and one more click to find. The four options sit at different points on that line. Cards per owner (the previous Contact first) do not scale: five owners and forty officers is a page of cards.
  **What was built (a lab only, `app/proto/project-owners/`; nothing in the product changed).** `variants.tsx` (the four), `page.tsx` (the lab: a Layout tool for the options and a People tool for the states). The people data and the shared pieces are `app/proto/project-overview/` (`people.ts` gains "Five owners, forty people": 14, 9, 7, 6 and 4 officers; four placeholder people repeat with numbered addresses, CONTRACTS 0.3; `people-ui.tsx` gains a table without the Data owner column and a dialog that can take a search and an empty state). Registered in `app/proto/page.tsx` and `production-routes.ts`. (1) **Accordions, a line each:** the page's compact accordion (the Overview's sections use it), an owner is a row (name, a count, its primary contact in a line, "11 current, 3 not active") that opens to its primary contact in full and a line per officer, six at a time ("Show 4 more"), the ones not active folded; the first owner starts open; with more than two, "Expand all". The contact line goes while the row is open, because the body then says it first (4.3). (2) **Accordions, names then a card:** the same rows, the officers as names (about ten in two lines), each opening a small card (role, status, email, phone) in the design system's popover; not active under their own fold. The densest. (3) **Contacts table:** one line per owner with who to contact and how (name, Primary, role, email, phone), always in view; "14 officers" opens that owner's officers in a dialog with a search (from six officers up). The answer first, the people on request. (4) **Directory:** every officer in one table with the product's own search, Filter (data owner, status, role) and chips, paged past ten; current first with each owner's primary contact leading, so page one answers who to contact.
  **Decided by the agent, to confirm.** Status is Current or Not active as before, held in the lab. In option 2 status is said by the group a name is in ("Other current officers", "Not active") and in the card, not on every chip. A person who is not active has no mail link. A list that grows is bounded at six with a "Show N more" and not scrolled (4.2d). The open owner's header drops its contact line (said once). The owner order is the order in Add Project.
  **Open.** (1) Which of the four, or a mix: the accordion rows are the safest to fold into the page (the page already has the pattern), and the Directory the safest for a project with dozens of officers; a hybrid is possible (an accordion of owners, a Directory when an owner has more than about twelve). (2) Whether names (option 2) are findable enough: the role is behind a press. (3) Whether the Contacts table should show a status for the contact (it shows only current ones, so it would always read Current). (4) Where status is set (CONTRACTS 4.7 and `-17` open 3). (5) Whether "Expand all" is wanted. (6) The popover is built from the design system's `Popover` and react-aria's `DialogTrigger` in the lab; if chosen, a person card is a new pattern and would be made in `components/**` with the designer's override (1.4, 1.10), not kept in a screen.
