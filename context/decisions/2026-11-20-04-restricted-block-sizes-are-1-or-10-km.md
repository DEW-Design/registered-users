# 2026-11-20 - Restricted block sizes are 1 or 10 km, 10 km for the demo, and the legend drops Restricted block

- **Nov 20 2026: Changed in the product (`app/pages/_shared/map-search/search-data.ts`, `species-results.tsx`, `sensitive-restriction-report-data.ts`, `observations-search.tsx`).** The designer: remove "Restricted (block)" from the map key; "Restricted blocks can be 1km2 or 10km2. So, let's update the 5km2 logic to reflect either 1km2 or 10km2. For the purpose of the demo, let's tweak 5km2 to 10km2."
  - **Key.** The "Restricted (block)" entry is gone from the Explore map key. A restricted record's block is the colour of its flora or fauna and is named in its tooltip ("location restricted to a N km block"). The key now lists Flora and Fauna (and "Other record" when plotted).
  - **Block sizes.** `restrictedRadiusKm` (the one rule the Species table, the Explore map and the record page share) returned 5 for a bird and 10 for everything else. It now returns the size a record carries, **1 or 10** (`RestrictedBlockKm`, optional `restrictedBlockKm` on an occurrence), and otherwise `DEFAULT_RESTRICTED_BLOCK_KM`, which is **10**. So for the demo every restricted record is a 10 km block, birds included, and a record can be made a 1 km block by setting the one field; none is set. The two other places that carried the literal 10 (`obfuscationRadiusFor`, the sensitive restriction report) use the default, and their comments (which said "birds 5 km") are corrected.
  - **Read as.** "1km2 or 10km2" as the block's side (1 km or 10 km), which is how the code and the text on screen ("a 10 km block") already measure it; a 10 km block is 100 square km. If the business means areas (1 and 10 square km, about 1 km and 3.2 km a side), the two numbers change.
  - **Measured (20 Nov 2026, Chromium headless).** The key for a search of "malleefowl" reads "SPECIES GROUP, Fauna" with no restricted entry. Zoomed in four levels with the scale bar at 20 km = 77px (3.85px per km): the restricted Malleefowl block is 33 by 39px, about 10 km a side (it was about 19px at 5 km); a public Heath Mouse is a 12px dot. Zero console errors; `tsc`, `eslint --max-warnings=0` on `app/pages` (one warning in `biodata-home/page.tsx`, not from this work) and `check:contracts` clean.
  - **Not checked.** The record page's "10 km block" text and the Species table's coordinate cell for a restricted bird (they read the same function; not opened); the project page's restricted records (they take `blockKm` from it); a 1 km record (none is set, so the 1 km path was not run).
  - **Open.** Which records should be 1 km and which 10 km, once the business says (one field on the occurrence). The project page's record map has its own 11.1 km block for a record with no size (`RESTRICTED_BLOCK_KM`); it was not changed.
