# 2026-11-19 - The map slides instead of flying: no zoom-out arc

- **Nov 19 2026: Fixed in the product (`app/pages/_shared/map-search/sa-map.tsx`, `components/custom/draw-map/use-drawing.ts`).** The designer, with a screen recording of Explore: "I hate the map flyby - it's so weird, happens even when i click on a species. it's a very unpleasant movement." The recording (a polygon on the map, the area's chip pressed) shows the view zooming out and back in on its way to the area.
  - **Cause.** The map moved with Leaflet's `flyTo` and `flyToBounds`, which zoom out and back in along an arc (a "flight") whatever the distance, even to move to a point at the same zoom. Three paths used them: fitting the map to the search areas whenever they change, "zoom to this area" (pressing an area's chip or its shape), and centring a clicked record's dot (a species or a record), which asked for a pan only but was made with `flyTo` at the same zoom, so it still arced.
  - **Now.** A fit to areas and "zoom to this area" are `fitBounds` with the same padding, animated for 0.4 seconds: a smooth zoom and slide. Centring a record's dot is `panTo`: the zoom is not touched at all. The project extent's "go to a place" (`flyTo` in the drawing state) is `setView`, animated. A move too long for Leaflet to animate (more than four zoom levels) is a cut, not an arc; the first fit of a map is still a jump, as before. Where the person has asked for reduced motion, none of these animate (they were not checking it). The map's own wheel and button zoom are unchanged.
  - **Measured (19 Nov 2026, Chromium headless, 1600 by 950, sampled every frame).** Pressing an area's chip after the map was dragged away and zoomed in a level: the shape's width went from 1232px to 616px and never below 616px (an arc would pass under it), its centre from (736, 374) to (1032, 536). Clicking a species in the list with the map dragged off: the shape's width stayed 966px for all 83 frames (no zoom change), its centre moved from (695, 608) to (696, 524), a pan. Zero console errors. `tsc`, `eslint --max-warnings=0` on `app/pages` and `components/custom` (one warning in `biodata-home/page.tsx`, not from this work) and `check:contracts` clean.
  - **Agent calls (reversible: `MOVE_SECONDS` and `canAnimate` in `sa-map.tsx`).** (1) 0.4 seconds, not the flight's 0.5 or 0.6. (2) A cut for a very long move rather than any animation: it is what Leaflet does when it cannot animate the move, and the alternative is the arc you dislike. (3) Every map that uses `SAMap` changes with it (the nomination, DLA and record pickers, the project page's map), since they share the three paths.
  - **Not checked.** The feel itself (I measured the path, not the look); a fit that crosses many zoom levels (the cut); the project extent's place search; touch; reduced motion on (the code path, not run).
  - **Open.** Whether a long move should be animated in two steps instead of a cut. Whether the map should move at all when a species is clicked and its dot is already in view (a pan then moves it to the centre of the visible part, by an earlier request: "the dot needs to be centred always").
  - **Touches:** contracts 1.9 (reduced motion, 8), 2.9; `2026-11-19-11`, `-12`.
