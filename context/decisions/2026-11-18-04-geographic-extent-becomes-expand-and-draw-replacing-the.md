# 2026-11-18 - Geographic extent becomes Expand and draw, replacing the four tabs

- **Nov 18 2026: Decided, in direction. Not built in the product yet.** The designer, answering the first open question of `2026-11-18-03` ("Should Expand and draw replace the form's four tabs, so the form never holds a map to draw on?"): "Correct, I think it makes sense to do this." So Add Project's Geographic extent row will hold a summary of the area and one button (Expand and draw, Expand and edit), and the drawing, the other ways of adding an area and the coordinates are done in a workspace that takes the window.
  - **Decided (the designer's word).** The form does not hold a map to draw on, and the four tabs (Draw on the Map, Choose from a List, Coordinates, Upload Shapefile) are not kept on the form. The shape of the idea is `/proto/expand-and-draw` (`2026-11-18-03`).
  - **What follows from it, and is not yet decided.** (1) *Which workspace.* The four ways of adding an area must live somewhere once the tabs are gone. Of the two workspaces in the lab, only "Split" has all four (as tabs in its panel); "On the map" has drawing only, so choosing it would mean adding place, paste and file to it. (2) *One area or several.* The lab lets a project hold any number of areas (the tabs held one shape). If more than one is wanted, `GeoExtentValue` (`app/pages/project-registration/types.ts`), the review step, the project page, the reports that read an extent and every project already saved in a browser change with it; if one is enough, the workspace would keep a single shape. (3) *Where it starts.* The workspace opens on the whole state until there is an area. (4) *Cancel* with unsaved areas. These are the open items of `2026-11-18-03`; nothing here settles them.
  - **Supersedes nothing.** The picker's four tabs, `GeoExtentPicker`, stay as they are until the product change is made; `2026-10-09` (version 2's Expand dialog on the map) is the nearest earlier decision and is built on, not reversed.
  - **Touches:** contracts 4.1, 1.10 (the drawing engine is built in the lab and has to move to `components/**` with the designer's override when it is built), 5.4; `2026-11-18-03`.
