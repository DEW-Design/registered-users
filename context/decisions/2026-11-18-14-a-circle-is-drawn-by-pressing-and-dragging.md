# 2026-11-18 - A circle is drawn by pressing and dragging out, with the handle on the radius

- **Nov 18 2026: Changed in the lab (`app/proto/map-drawing/draw-map.tsx`, `drawing.ts`, `ui.tsx`). Nothing in the product.** The designer: "Drag to add a circle was more intuitive, then having the handle on the radius would be nice :)" The product's own circle (`leaflet-draw`) is pressed at the centre and dragged out in one move; the lab's engine had replaced it with a click for the centre and a second click for the edge, which is two gestures and gives no sign of the size until the second.
  - **Now.** (1) *One gesture.* Press for the centre, drag out, let go when it is the size you want. The prompt says so ("Press for the centre and drag out to the size you want.", then "Let go when it is the size you want."). (2) *A handle on the radius while it is drawn.* A line runs from the centre to the pointer, a handle sits at the pointer, and the radius is written beside it ("6.8 km"), with the circle the pointer would make drawn dashed; the header's facts read the same radius and its area live ("Radius 6.8 km, Area 145 km²"). (3) *The handle stays where it was pulled to.* After letting go, the radius handle is on the circle in the direction it was dragged (up and to the left in the check), not moved to due east as it was; it is kept per area (`bearingOf`, `setBearing`) and follows the pointer when it is dragged afterwards (dragged 60 right and 150 down: it moved 60 and 150, and the radius went 6.8 to 5.5 km). (4) *The map does not pan while the circle tool is on* (checked: the map pane's transform unchanged during the drag), since a press would be a pan; the zoom buttons and the wheel still work. (5) *The two-click way still works:* a press that does not move more than 6px is a click, so click for the centre then click for the edge draws one (checked: 5.1 km), which is how a touch screen draws it, since the browser sends no mouse events for a finger moving. (6) *Escape while dragging cancels it,* and letting go afterwards draws nothing (checked: no centre marker, no circle left). Letting go outside the map still finishes it (the release is listened for on the window).
  - **A fault found and fixed while building it.** The release handler was made at the press, when no centre was set, so at the release it set a centre of radius 0 instead of finishing the circle (`Radius 0 km`, the prompt stuck at "Let go"). It now reads the latest controller (`latest`).
  - **Agent calls (each reversible in `draw-map.tsx`).** The 6px that makes a press a drag; the line from the centre to the pointer and the tooltip's wording ("6.8 km"); a ring on the handle while held (the same as the polygon's closing point); the direction kept per area in the controller and not in the area's data (a `Boundary` has no direction), so it is not saved with a project: a circle opened again from storage has its handle due east.
  - **Open, for the designer.** Whether the radius handle should keep its direction in the saved area (a small addition to the data) or always sit due east when a project is opened again. Whether a finger should draw by dragging too (the lab uses taps there, since Leaflet gives no drag events for a finger on its own); that needs touch events handled by the engine. Whether the radius label should show on the shape after drawing (it shows in the header and the table now).
  - **Verified (18 Nov 2026, Chromium headless, a real mouse):** zero console errors. Press at one point and drag up and left: "Let go when it is the size you want.", "Radius 6.8 km, Area 145 km²", the label "6.8 km", no pan; released: "Check the area you drew.", "Shape Circle Radius 6.8 km Area 145 km²", centre and radius handles, the radius handle up and left of the centre. Two clicks: Radius 5.1 km. Escape mid-drag: nothing left. Earlier checks pass (point dragging 182 of 184px with no frame over 17ms, a circle's centre dragged with its radius handle along, the cursor modes, the Edit chip, every area editable in the workspace).
