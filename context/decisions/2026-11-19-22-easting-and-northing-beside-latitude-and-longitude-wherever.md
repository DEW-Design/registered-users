# 2026-11-19 - Easting and northing beside latitude and longitude wherever coordinates are entered

- **Nov 19 2026: Easting and northing beside latitude and longitude wherever coordinates are entered.** The designer: "Through out the application where we provide coordinates, the user must also be able to provide easting and northing. Add a radio button on top to let the user choose what to provide, easting northing or lat long" (screenshot: Add Project, Geographic extent, Coordinates tab).
  - **One control, `CoordinateFields`** (`app/pages/_shared/coordinate-fields.tsx`), built from the design system's `RadioGroup`, `Select` and `InputNumber`: a radio on top ("Latitude & Longitude", "Easting & Northing"), then Latitude and Longitude, or Zone, Easting and Northing. Whatever is entered, the caller holds latitude and longitude only, so no record, boundary or search changed shape. Switching the choice carries the place over (a latitude and longitude shows as its grid position, and the other way round). Borrowed from the radio the DLA "Add a location" modal already had (0.9); that modal now uses the shared control, so there is one.
  - **Used by:** Add Project's geographic extent and every location restriction (`GeoExtentPicker`, so also a record's Location card in the project page), a Point intercept's point (`LocationField pointOnly`), the DLA "Add a location" modal, the survey record drawer (`record-form.tsx`, Latitude and Longitude keep their `dwc:` hints), and Explore's "Enter coordinates".
  - **Conversion** is `mgaToLatLon` (`app/pages/_shared/mga.ts`, proj4, GRS80 UTM south) one way and the existing `toMga2020` (`location-details-table.tsx`) the other. `mga.ts` is split out of `dla/dla-geo.ts` so a form that only takes coordinates does not load the shapefile reader; `dla-geo` lost its zone 54 helper.
  - **Zone is a field (decision for the designer to confirm, 0.4).** A grid position means nothing without its zone, and South Australia spans MGA zones 52, 53 and 54. The DLA modal had assumed zone 54 and documented that as a simplification; the zone is now a small Select (52, 53, 54, default 54), the same "Zone" the Location details table already shows. If the designer wants no Zone field, the default is 54 and the field is one block to remove.
  - **Precision.** The degree fields step by 0.00001 (about a metre) in every caller, where the picker, the modal and Explore used 0.01: a number field shows and snaps to its step, so a latitude converted from an easting showed as "-34.95" for -34.94644. Eastings and northings are whole metres, no thousands separator.
  - **Behaviour changes to note.** Clearing latitude or longitude in the Add Project picker now clears the extent (before, a half-entered point was given a made-up longitude of 138 or latitude of -34.93). Explore's "Add point" keeps the chosen system and clears the fields (before, the fields remounted). Explore's radius now sits beside "Add point" on its own row.
  - **Datum.** MGA is taken as GDA2020 (as `toMga2020` already does); no datum choice is offered.
  - **Verified live (localhost:3000):** Add Project > Geographic extent (the screenshot's -35.79, 139.29, 26 km; E/N 345467, 6037994 in zone 54; 270000, 6130000 gives -34.94644, 138.48142; blur without change does not move it; clearing and retyping a field), the DLA modal (Add location enabled from E/N), Explore (Add point from E/N made "Point at -34.94644, 138.48142"), and a record's Location card in the project page (compact tabs). `tsc`, `eslint` on every touched file and `check:contracts` clean; no console errors on those screens.
  - **Not run:** the Point intercept's point-only field and the survey record drawer (`RecordDrawer`, the form flagged unused in 3.14) were typechecked and linted but not opened; no touch or keyboard pass on the new radio and zone select; no automated test of the two-way conversion beyond the values above.
  - **Open:** whether the Zone field stays (above); whether a hint naming the datum (MGA2020) should sit under the grid fields; the old narrow-column layout of Zone, Easting and Northing in a compact picker (checked at 1168px wide only).
