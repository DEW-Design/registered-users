# 2026-11-17 - Geographic extent exploration at proto, four ways to capture a project's area

- **Nov 17 2026: Exploring. Nothing is decided.** The designer, on a screenshot of the Geographic extent picker (four tabs, one method at a time, one shape): "is there a better way to capture information from the user? I want to draw, add coordinates (not just one), choose from a list or upload a shapefile. Can I see a few options? Could just be on proto." The direction is open and no clause settles it, so it is explored (the context-log rule for an unsettled direction), not picked. Route: `/proto/geographic-extent` (`app/proto/geographic-extent/`), indexed at `/proto` (`app/proto/page.tsx`). Keys: 1 to 4 and the arrows switch; the choice is kept in `?v=`.
  - **The four variants, each with its axis.**
    1. *Current.* The picker as it ships (`GeoExtentPicker`, imported live, so it stays the baseline once anything is integrated). Four tabs, one method at a time, one shape.
    2. *Areas.* Axis: the model. A project's extent is a list of areas, each added by any method (draw a polygon or a circle, choose a place, enter coordinates, upload a file), so one project can be two polygons, a place and three points. Every area is a row that can be removed, and pointing at a row draws its shape heavier on the map.
    3. *Coordinates grid.* Axis: data first. A table of latitude and longitude rows you type, paste by the dozen or fill by drawing a polygon; "one area" (a polygon from three or more points) or "separate points" (each with a radius). The map is the live preview. A row outside South Australia says so on the row.
    4. *One box.* Axis: intent. One field takes a place name, a pasted list of coordinates or a dropped file, works out which it is and offers the one thing that fits ("Add as one area", "Add as 8 points", a place's Add). Drawing sits beside it. What is added is a row of removable chips.
  - **Built from what is already there, not new.** The product's map (`sa-map.tsx`, which already draws any number of areas, as Explore's areas list does), its shapefile reader (`parseShapefileUpload`, which returns every polygon and point; the picker keeps only the first), the Named location list, the design system's `Button`, `Input`, `InputNumber`, `InputFile`, `TextArea`, `Select` and `RadioGroup`. Only the picker pill (the harness's own chrome) and the lab's pieces are new, all under `app/proto` (5.4).
  - **Agent calls (each reversible by deleting `app/proto`).** The set of four and their axes; the file types offered in the lab (`.geojson`, `.json`, `.zip`, `.shp`; the product offers `.geojson` and `.shp`); a point read from a file gets the registration radius, 25 km, as the picker's own coordinates tab does; a pasted "longitude, latitude" pair is turned round when only that way it falls inside South Australia.
  - **What each costs, honestly.** *Current:* one method at a time, one shape only, and at 320 wide its four tabs do not fit (the document is 320px wider than the window; found here, not caused by the lab). *Areas:* the saved extent stops being one value, so `GeoExtentValue`, the review step, the project page and the reports that read an extent would all change to hold a list. *Coordinates grid:* quickest for people who already hold their points as a list, but the most like a form of a spreadsheet and the slowest for someone who would just point at the map. *One box:* least to explain for the common case, but it hides what it can take until you type or drag, and a pasted list that looks like numbers but is not coordinates is told off only after the paste.
  - **Open, for the designer.** Which direction, or which parts of which (for example Areas' list with the One box's field above it). Whether a project should be able to hold more than one area at all, which is the real cost, since the data and every screen that reads an extent change with it. Whether the shapefile reader should take every polygon and point (as here) or the first only (as the product does). Until chosen, nothing in the product changes.
  - **A lab does not ship (5.4), and this repo has no step that removes it.** The prototype repo's Pages workflows remove `app/proto` before `next build`. This edition has no workflows, so `npm run build` would include `/proto` in the static site that test participants get. Before a build for participants, `app/proto` has to be removed or moved out; or the designer may want a build step here that does it. Not changed.
  - **Verified (17 Nov 2026, `http://localhost:3000/proto/geographic-extent`, Chromium headless):** zero console errors across the four variants at 320, 768 and 1440 wide. *Areas:* a coordinate point, a place and a GeoJSON file of two polygons and a point were added: "5 areas (2 polygons, 3 circles)". *Coordinates grid:* eight pasted points became eight rows and "Polygon of 8 points" on the map. *One box:* typing "Coor" offered places; the same eight lines gave "Add as one area"; the file added three more: four chips. The picker: key 3 went to `?v=3`, the right arrow to `?v=4`. At 768 none of the four overflows; at 320 variants 2 to 4 do not overflow and Current does. `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npx eslint app/proto` and `npm run check:contracts` pass.
  - **Not checked.** Drawing a polygon or a circle by mouse on the map, in any variant (the drawing code is the product's, reused, but no drag was run); a real `.zip` or `.shp` file (only GeoJSON was read); keyboard-only use and a screen reader; touch on a phone; the `emil-ui-review` pass over each variant; the type and spacing against 2.9 by `getComputedStyle`. Not committed.
  - **Touches:** contracts 5.4, 4.1; the Add Project "Geographic extent" row (`form-sections.tsx`), which is not changed.
