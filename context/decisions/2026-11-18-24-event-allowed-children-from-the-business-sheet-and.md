# 2026-11-18 - Event allowed children from the business sheet, and the parent shown when adding

- **Nov 18 2026: Event allowed children from the business sheet, and the parent shown when adding.**
  **The designer's brief.** "When a new event or occurrence or observation is added, the user must be aware where it will be added under. Best to retain how it shows here the parent details" (a screenshot of the record panel's header: type and ID, name, Parent, start date), and, with the business's sheet `Event_allowedchildrenevents.xlsx`: "Ensure this reflects on the application. Make sure this is added to the context."
  **What changed.** (a) `app/pages/project-detail/record-rules.ts`: `ALLOWED_CHILD_EVENTS` is the sheet's table, one row per event, and `allowedChildEvents`, `canContain` and `childOptions` read it, so every Add menu (a record's, the header's contextual "This project" list, Duplicate) and the store's `addRecord` check agree. It replaces "any event can have any event" and the rule that a Site sits only directly under the project. "*" in the sheet (Visit, Point intercept) is `NEEDS_PARENT_EVENT`: not offered directly under the project. The sheet's Trap Array is the app's Trap, its Custom is Custom event. (b) `record-full-view.tsx` and `record-header-meta.tsx`: a record being added shows the same header as one being viewed, with its Parent (the parent's ID and name, or the project's for a record directly under it) and, in the side panel, the ID after the type; the date is left out (`isNew`), since it is the field being filled in. (c) The table and its open points are in `.claude/rules/ref-domain.md`, "Which event may hold which".
  **Decided (designer, asked 18 Nov 2026).** Quadrat is not in the sheet: it follows Transect on both sides (`FOLLOWS`), offered wherever a Transect is and holding what a Transect holds.
  **Open, for the business.** The two "?" cells (a Trap inside a Ramble and inside a Transect) are allowed as listed. A Site inside another event is what the sheet says and is built, against the earlier model; to confirm. The data ingestion sandbox's tree and the `/config/data-model` parent checks still use the old shape and were not changed. Records already in the seed that the sheet would not allow are left as they are (the rule is applied when something is added).
  **Verified.** `npx tsc --noEmit` clean. A script reads the sheet's cells from the xlsx and compares them with `canContain` for every event type: all nine rows match, the project's list is every event but Visit and Point intercept, Quadrat follows Transect, occurrences and observations are allowed under every event. In the browser (project BD-5039, BioData Admin): the Add menu of a Trap lists Site, Visit, Trap, Opportune, Point intercept, Custom event; the header's Add under the project lists Site, Transect, Quadrat, Block, Ramble, Trap, Opportune, Custom event; adding a Visit under the Trap shows "EVENT-VISIT, VU00503", "New visit", "Parent TRP00501 - Pitfall and Elliott trap line A", in the same Barlow 14px label and value as a viewed record. Not verified: the full-screen (non-panel) add, an occurrence or observation add, and the other roles. Not committed.
