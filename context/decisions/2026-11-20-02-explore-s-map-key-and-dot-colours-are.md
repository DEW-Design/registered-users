# 2026-11-20 - Explore's map key and dot colours are flora and fauna

- **Nov 20 2026: Changed in the product (`app/pages/_shared/map-search/species-group-icons.ts`, `observations-search.tsx`, `record-detail.tsx`).** The designer, passing on the business: "Instead of Species group labels: we'll just have flora and fauna instead of the current ones. The business wanted to simplify this." The key under the Explore map listed Mammal, Bird, Amphibian, Plant (and Reptile) and the dots were coloured by those five groups. It now lists **Flora** and **Fauna** (whichever are in view, then "Other record" and "Restricted (block)" as before), and the dots are green (flora) or blue (fauna).
  - **The rule.** A Plant is flora; every other species group (Mammal, Bird, Reptile, Amphibian) is fauna (`lifeFormOf`). The five species groups are unchanged in the data and everywhere else: the species cards' icons, the Species group column and filters in the reports, the header search. Only the map's colour and key changed. The record page's restricted-block preview takes the same flora or fauna colour, so one record is one colour wherever it is shown (0.9).
  - **Colours.** Green for flora and orange for fauna (`--color-utility-green-500`, `--color-utility-orange-500`): the plant colour and the mammal colour before. The designer chose them ("Flora green, fauna orange like before"), after a first version in green and blue. Green beside orange is a pair that people with red-green colour blindness can mix up; the key names each colour, so colour is never the only cue (2.1 and the key's own rule).
  - **Measured (20 Nov 2026, Chromium headless, a search for "a").** The key reads "SPECIES GROUP, Flora, Fauna, Restricted (block)" on one line (it was two); the swatches are `rgb(23, 178, 106)`, `rgb(239, 104, 32)` and grey; the dots are the two utility colours (green for flora, orange for fauna) plus the brand dot of the search's area marker; no key on the Projects tab.
