# 2026-11-19 - Explore draws areas with the project's drawing tools: add a circle, add a polygon, corners to drag

- **Nov 19 2026: Built in the product (`components/custom/draw-map/draw-layer.tsx` new, `draw-map.tsx`, `app/pages/_shared/map-search/sa-map.tsx`, `app/pages/observations/observations-search.tsx`).** The designer: "the map tool updates we made on the project bits need to flow into the Explore tab as well. Mainly the add a circle and add a polygon with more control to select corners, etc."
  - **What Explore does now.** "Add area" offers **Add a circle** and **Add a polygon** (they read "Draw a ..."; the shape icons are kept, 2.4). A polygon is placed corner by corner (click; Enter, a double click or the first corner finishes it; Undo takes the last back; the corners drag, with a number and its coordinates beside the pointer). A circle is pressed out from its centre in one gesture, with the radius beside the pointer. A finished shape is **held for review**, as the project's geographic extent holds it: its corners (a polygon) or its centre and radius (a circle) can be dragged, a small dot on an edge adds a corner, and a hover on a corner shows its number and coordinates. The bar on the map says each step and has the buttons: while placing, Undo (a polygon with a corner placed) and Cancel; in review, Redraw, **Add area** (primary) and Cancel; Escape puts down a drawing or a held shape. **Add area** adds it as a search area exactly as before (a chip in the list, results and map narrowed to it).
  - **How (1.10, 0.9).** The same engine and state as the project's extent (OVR-028, OVR-029), in a thin layer (`DrawLayer`, OVR-031) that sits inside Explore's own map. The map keeps everything it draws (the areas, parks and shapefiles, the result dots, the legend); the layer draws only the shape being made. The drawing state lives in the layer, so moving the pointer does not re-render Explore; Explore is told only the tool, the kind held for review and the number of corners placed, and gives commands. `SAMap` gets opt-in props (`drawRef`, `onDrawState`, `drawReview`); without `drawRef` a map keeps leaflet-draw exactly as before, so **no other map changed**.
  - **Measured (19 Nov 2026, Chromium headless, 1600 by 950).** Menu: Add a circle, Add a polygon, Enter coordinates, Choose national parks, Upload a shapefile. Polygon: bar text for 0, 1, 3 corners and review; 4 corner handles; a corner dragged from (1216, 370) to (1276, 330) with the pointer's 60 and -40 moves; "Add area" adds the chip "POLYGON 1" and the bar goes. Circle: 86.9 km shown while dragging, review with 2 handles, Escape puts it down and leaves the earlier area. A search of "a" with the page at 4x CPU slowdown while the pointer moves through a drawing: frame interval median 17ms, p95 17ms, max 17ms (60 frames). Zero console errors. `tsc`, `eslint --max-warnings=0` on `app/pages` and `components/custom` (one warning in `biodata-home/page.tsx`, not from this work) and `check:contracts` clean.
  - **Agent calls (reversible: one prop on `SAMap`, or the bar).** (1) The shape is held for review before it is added (the project's "check it first"), not added the moment it is finished: it gives corner control at once, at the cost of one more press. (2) The bar's copy follows the project's ("Click the map to place the first corner", "Press for the centre and drag out to the size you want"; "corner" for "point"). (3) The override for `draw-layer.tsx` (OVR-031) was recorded on your request to bring the tools into Explore, as OVR-028 to OVR-030 were.
  - **Not done, on purpose.** An area already added cannot be edited by its corners: it is removed and drawn again, as before. That needs a way to start editing (an Edit action on its chip or its shape) and to end it, which is a design choice (a press on a shape zooms to it today); proposed, not built. The other maps that draw (nominations, DLA's add a location, the project record forms, the project page's older picker) still use leaflet-draw.
  - **Not checked.** A touch screen (a circle by two taps, a finger on a corner); Explore's second layout and the Map and table view while drawing; drawing with a keyword search and results open, other than the frame measure; Explore's restored saved search with a drawn area; screen reader on the bar.
  - **Open, for the designer.** Edit an added area by its corners (and how it starts). Move the other maps that draw to the same tools (the nomination and DLA forms). Whether the bar's "Add area" should be named differently from the card's "Add area" menu (the two share a label).
