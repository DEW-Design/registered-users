# 2026-11-18 - Map drawing flow exploration at proto, where the coordinates live

- **Nov 18 2026: Exploring. Nothing is decided.** The designer, trying the "Areas" variant of `2026-11-17-11`, with a screenshot: "select an option, draw and see what the coords are - doesn't seem to flow, the experience specifically. Can we please look into this? Potentially coming up with designs for an optimal map UX?" The direction is open and no clause settles it, so it is explored, not picked. Route: `/proto/map-drawing` (`app/proto/map-drawing/`), in the `/proto` index. Keys 1 to 4 and the arrows switch; `?v=` keeps the choice.
  - **What does not flow in the current one, found by drawing in it with a mouse (the first time any drawing was run).** (1) While you draw, the page still says "No areas yet"; the only help is a tooltip that follows the pointer. (2) Nothing says how many points there are, how big the shape is or what its radius is, until it is finished. (3) There is no Undo, no Cancel and no Finish: a wrong point is a lost shape. (4) When it is finished, the shape becomes a row under the map, "Polygon, 3 points", far from where the eye is; its coordinates are not shown anywhere and cannot be edited. (5) You cannot move a point after drawing, only delete the shape and start again. (6) The map starts at the whole state (a 200 km scale bar), so an accurate shape needs a search and a zoom first that the screen does not offer. (7) The tool buttons are above the map and the result is below it, so the three steps are in three places.
  - **The four variants, each with its axis.**
    1. *Current.* `AreasVariant` of `/proto/geographic-extent`, imported live, as the designer used it.
    2. *Panel.* Axis: where the numbers live, beside the map. A panel over the map (under it on a phone) that says where you are in the job: choosing a shape; drawing, with the points filling a table as you click, the live count and area, Undo, Cancel and Finish; or looking at a shape, with the same table editable. A "go to a place or coordinates" box is at its head.
    3. *On the map.* Axis: no chrome beside the map. A tool column on the map's edge, one pill at the top that says what to do next with the live numbers and Undo and Cancel, the pointer's coordinates in a corner, and a card on the map for the selected shape holding its coordinates, with Copy, Delete and Done. The areas are chips under the map.
    4. *Check it first.* Axis: committing. Choose, draw and check are three steps with their own header. A finished shape is not added: it waits, dashed, with a sheet over the foot of the map showing its coordinates (editable, and the points drag), and Redraw or Use this area. The map moves so the shape sits above the sheet.
  - **What all three new ones do that the current one does not.** The instruction changes with where you are ("Click the map to place the first point", "Click the first point, double-click or press Enter to finish"); the points, the area in km² and the circle's radius are live while drawing; the pointer's coordinates are shown; Enter finishes, Backspace takes the last point back, Escape cancels; a finished shape can be selected and its points dragged (a circle: the centre and a radius handle), and the numbers follow the drag; the coordinates can be typed (a half-typed "-3" is not an error, a value outside South Australia is flagged on the field, and it is taken or put back when you leave it); a place or "lat, lon" can be flown to before drawing.
  - **Built from what is already there.** The product's tiles and bounds, `MapZoomButtons`, the Named location list, the design system's `Button`, `ButtonUtility`, `Input`. The drawing engine (`draw-map.tsx`) is new and is the lab's own: the product's `SAMap` wraps `leaflet-draw`, which gives no live numbers and no handles.
  - **Agent calls (each reversible by deleting `app/proto/map-drawing`).** The set of four and their axes; the instruction wording; a circle is drawn by clicking the centre and then the edge, with the radius live (the product's draws by press and drag); a finished shape ends the tool (it does not stay on for the next); points on a polygon cannot be fewer than three; the sample text and coordinate format ("lat, lon", five decimals).
  - **What each costs, honestly.** *Panel:* covers the left 18rem of a 690px map at the form's own width, so a shape cannot be drawn under it; it wants the Expand dialog's room, or a narrower panel. *On the map:* the most direct and the least text, but the coordinates are in a small card that can cover the shape's right edge, and the tools are icons, so they need their tooltips; the list of areas is secondary. *Check it first:* the safest and the clearest about what is added, but two more presses for every shape, and the sheet hides the foot of the map. All three: this engine is a new behaviour, so if one is chosen it moves into `components/**` with the designer's override (1.10, 1.4) and a doc page; it is not product code now.
  - **Open, for the designer.** Which direction, or which parts (for example the on-map pill and card, with Check it first's step for confirming). Whether drawing should keep the tool on for the next shape. Whether the map should open already zoomed (to the project's place, once named). Whether a shape may be drawn over the form's own width, or only in the expanded map. And the open items of `2026-11-17-11` still stand, including that a project holding several areas changes the data (`GeoExtentValue`) and every screen that reads it, and that a lab is not removed from the static build in this repo (5.4).
  - **Verified (18 Nov 2026, `http://localhost:3000/proto/map-drawing`, Chromium headless, a real mouse and keyboard):** zero console errors. *Panel:* three points placed read "3 points, 23 km²" and listed their coordinates; Enter finished it (4 points, 46 km²); typing -34.90 into point 1's latitude changed it; dragging a handle 60px changed point 2's longitude from 138.69450 to 138.69175. *On the map:* the pill read "3 points, 24 km²" with the pointer's coordinates live; clicking the first point finished the shape (found and fixed a fault here: the first point was made before it could close, so the click added a fifth point); the card listed the four coordinates. *Check it first:* a circle read "Radius 2.6 km, 21 km²" while drawing; the sheet showed latitude, longitude and radius; "Use this area" put it in the list. Keys: Backspace took a point back (3 to 2), Escape cancelled to the start. At 320 wide none of the three overflows, and the panel sits under the map, not over it. `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npx eslint app/proto` and `npm run check:contracts` pass.
