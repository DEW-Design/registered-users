# 2026-11-17 - Add Project discard prompt has two buttons, Discard and Cancel and keep editing

- **Nov 17 2026: The designer asked for the Add Project "Discard this project?" prompt to have two buttons, "Discard" and "Cancel & keep editing", not three** (with a screenshot of the prompt showing Keep editing, Save draft and Discard project). In `app/pages/project-registration/option-2/registration-flow.tsx` the `DestructiveModal` lost its `secondaryLabel` and `onSecondary` (Save draft), its confirm label went from "Discard project" to "Discard", and its cancel label from "Keep editing" to "Cancel & keep editing".
  - **Decided.** The two button labels and the removal of Save draft from the prompt are the designer's words. "Save draft" is still on the form's own footer (4.1 item 5), so a draft can still be saved before leaving; only the prompt no longer offers it.
  - **Agent call: the description.** It read "You have unsaved details. Save a draft to keep them, or discard them.", which would offer an action the prompt no longer has. It now reads "You have unsaved details. Discard them, or cancel and keep editing.", built from the prompt's own words. Reverse: replace the `description` prop in `registration-flow.tsx`; name the sentence you want.
  - **Agent call: the layout.** `DestructiveModal` draws the stacked layout (icon above the title, two buttons side by side at 768 and wider, one above the other at 320) when it has no secondary action, as every other discard and delete prompt does; the three-button version used its horizontal layout. Not chosen by hand: it follows from there being two buttons. Reverse: none is offered by the component, so a different layout would be a change to `DestructiveModal` (1.6), for the designer.
  - **Copy.** The label is the designer's "Cancel & Keep Editing" in sentence case (2.18, the principle of sentence case in `ref-principles.md`); the ampersand is the designer's, 2.3 does not forbid it. Icons unchanged: `ArrowLeft` on the cancel (3.12: keep editing, back out of a prompt), `Trash01` on Discard.
  - **Open, for the designer.** Five other forms have the same three-button prompt with "Save draft": DLA (`dla-form.tsx`), Sensitive species nomination (`nomination-form.tsx`), DSA (`dsa-form.tsx`), Controlled vocabulary (`cv-form.tsx`) and Notifications (`nt-form.tsx`); only the first two are in this edition's routes. 0.9 asks that two screens doing the same job match, so the screen being touched is brought in line with the others unless the designer decides otherwise; here the designer chose to change one. Whether the others follow, and whether the "Cancel & keep editing" wording is the product's from now on (3.12 names the action "Keep editing"), is for the designer.
  - **Verified (17 Nov 2026, live `/pages/project-registration/`, a name typed, the form's Close pressed, Chromium headless):** at 320, 768 and 1440 wide, zero console errors; the dialog has exactly two text buttons, "Cancel & keep editing" (white, `ArrowLeft`) and "Discard" (red, `Trash01`); no horizontal overflow; at 320 each button is 243px wide, one above the other. `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npx eslint` on the file and `npm run check:contracts` pass.
  - **Not checked.** What Discard and Cancel & keep editing then do were not pressed (the handlers were not touched: Discard still goes to the project list); the look in the prompt at the other forms; a real phone.
  - **Touches:** contracts 4.1 item 6, 3.12, 0.9; `2026-11-17-04`.
