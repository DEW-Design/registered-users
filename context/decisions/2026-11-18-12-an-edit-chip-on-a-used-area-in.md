# 2026-11-18 - An Edit chip on a used area in the form's map

- **Nov 18 2026: Changed in the lab (`app/proto/map-drawing/draw-map.tsx`, `app/proto/expand-and-draw/step-map.tsx`). Nothing in the product.** The designer, with a screenshot of the workspace as it was before `2026-11-18-11`, an "EDIT" label drawn on the middle of the shape: "something like this would be nice?" It answers the first open question of `-11`: how a used area in the form's map is edited without going to the list.
  - **What it is.** A small chip, a pencil and "Edit", on the middle of every used area in the form's map. Pressing it, or the shape, takes the area back to be checked: the step reads "Check the area you drew.", its points show (4 for the polygon tried), and "Use this area" puts it back. This is the same "check it first" way of editing as the list's Edit button, reached from the shape where the pointer already is.
  - **When it shows.** While the pointer is over the map (and when focus is in it), with a 150ms fade, nothing else moving; always on a screen with no hover (checked on a 390px touch device: opacity 1, `hover: none`). At rest it is hidden, so an area is not covered in labels (checked: opacity 0 at rest, 1 while hovering). It is not drawn while drawing or while a shape is being checked, and not in the workspace, where every area already shows its points (`-11`; checked: 0 chips and 4 points in the dialog). It is a tab stop with a name ("Edit this area").
  - **Agent calls (each reversible: `editChips` on `DrawMap`, the `.draw-edit-chip` rules).** The chip's look (white, 12px semibold, pencil icon and "Edit", the colours of the map's other floating controls, 63 by 26px); at the middle of the shape (the mean of a polygon's points, a circle's centre), which for a concave shape may fall outside it; pressing the shape itself does the same as the chip in the form; revealed with the same rule as Expand.
  - **Open, for the designer.** With several areas close together the chips can overlap; they could be placed by the area's name or only show for the area under the pointer. Whether pressing the shape in the form should do this (it does) or only select it. Whether "Edit" should read "Edit area" where the shape is not named.
  - **Verified (18 Nov 2026, `http://localhost:3000/proto/expand-and-draw?v=2`, Chromium headless):** zero console errors. A polygon drawn and used showed 1 chip, hidden at rest and visible over the map, text "Edit", cursor pointer. Pressing it: "Check the area you drew.", 4 points, no chip, "Use this area" present; using it and then pressing the shape did the same. Dialog: 0 chips. Touch: visible without hover.
  - **Not checked.** Two or more used areas near each other; a very thin or concave shape; keyboard-only use of the chip (it is focusable and shows on focus, not driven by Enter in a test); a screen reader.
