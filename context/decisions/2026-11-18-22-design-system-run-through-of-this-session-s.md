- **Nov 18 2026: Audit and fixes in the lab (`app/proto/**`). Nothing in the product.** The designer, with a screenshot of the expanded workspace in the review step: "too many buttons to take an action - overwhelming and confusing? Can we sense check this?" and "you've violated basic contracts (please do a run through) - you need to run a design system check." The `design-consistency` skill was run, scoped to what this session touched (the lab's map, step header, areas panel and dialog), not the whole repo. `/emil-typography` and `/emil-design-foundations` were run for 0.6 item 8, which had been skipped for this session's text changes. The findings are here and not in a new `audit/` file, because the audit is of one session's work.

A live pass in Chromium (1440 by 900) of the workspace in the review step: every pressable control counted, computed type per role, every colour compared with `contracts/figma-colours.json`, zero console errors.
  - **Violations found and fixed on sight.**
    1. **2.1, tokens only.** The map's dots, the edge dots, the Edit chip and the point tooltip carried literals (`#fff`, `#1a1a1a`, `#f4f4f4`, `rgba(0,0,0,.12/.2/.35)`) and the tooltip carried Leaflet's own `#222` text. All are now tokens (`--ui-bg-primary`, `--ui-bg-primary_hover`, `--ui-text-primary`, `--ui-border-primary`, `--ui-border-secondary`, `--shadow-sm`, `--shadow-md`). Measured: the tooltip is `#fff` on `#2e2925` text with a `#e5e4e2` border; of the colours on the review step the only two that are not in the Figma set are not from this session (a 12% border and a 60% white icon inside the design system's own buttons).
    2. **2.9, one role, one treatment.** The step eyebrow was `text-tertiary`; the product's eyebrows (`review-view.tsx`, `record-inspector.tsx`) are `text-xs font-semibold tracking-wide text-quaternary uppercase`. Brought in line, in both headers. Measured 12px/16px, 600, 0.3px tracking, uppercase.
    3. **2.12 and 4.3, a fact said once.** The dialog header still said "A new polygon to check" after the card said "Not added yet" (the word the designer had asked about): now "A new polygon, not added yet". The card's line "Use it from the bar under the map, or draw it again." explained controls that are next to it: removed.
    4. **One name for the step.** The line under "Step 3: Review" said "Check the area you drew." and the earlier variant "Check the coordinates.": both say "Review".
    5. **2.16, a hit area.** The Edit chip (24px tall) had none; it now has a 40px target (an invisible box 8px beyond it above and below).
  - **Found and not fixed (each needs the designer, 0.4).**
    1. **1.2, 1.4 and 1.10: `NumberBubble` is a lookalike.** The steps' number bubble exists only inside `IconTopNumber`; the lab draws its own (`number-bubble.tsx`). The designer asked for "the steps bubble", so this is deliberate, but it is a second implementation. If it goes into the product it must be the design system's: the bubble is lifted out of `IconTopNumber` as an opt-in export (a new component, 1.4, with the override record) and both use it.

    4. **3.12, the shape buttons take the shape icon, not the plus** (asked in `-19`, still open).
    5. **The coordinates table** shows 4 rows and the top of a fifth (`max-h-48`), so a row is cut in half at rest. It scrolls (the drawn scrollbar), but a cut row reads as an accident (2.12). A fix is to size it to whole rows or fade the foot like `ScrollFade`.
  - **The action count (the sense check).** In the review step with 6 points, 20 buttons are on screen outside the map, plus 3 tabs and 6 handles and the map zoom: **3 filled primaries** ("Use and finish" in the header, "Use this area" under the map, "Add place" in the panel), 3 secondaries, and **14 ghost icon buttons** (a magnifier and a bin on each of 6 rows, plus the card's bin). It grows with the shape: 2 per point plus 8. The foundations check says one primary per view; here two different "use" verbs with two different scopes (add this shape; close the workspace) are both filled, and there are three ways to lose the new shape: Redraw, the card's bin, and Cancel (which also drops the other areas' changes). Each fact is also said twice (6 points and 359,443 km² are in the step header's facts row and in the card's line).
  - **Recommended (not built: it changes what is offered, so it waits for the designer, 0.4).** (1) While a shape is waiting, the header's "Use and finish" is not a filled button: "Use this area" is the one primary; "Use and finish" is filled again when nothing is waiting. (2) While a shape is waiting, "Add an area" (tabs, place, Add place) is locked and dimmed, as the other cards already are. (3) The per-row magnifier goes (hovering a row now lights its point and shows where it is), so a row has one control, Remove: 6 fewer buttons for 6 points. (4) One way to lose the new shape in the panel: keep Redraw under the map and the card's bin, since the bin is what the designer asked for on 18 Nov for consistency; Cancel stays, in the header, as the whole workspace's. Result in this state: 1 primary, no icon duplicates, about 10 buttons for 6 points instead of 20.
