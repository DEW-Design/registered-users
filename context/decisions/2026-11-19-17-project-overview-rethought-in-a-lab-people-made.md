# 2026-11-19 - Project Overview rethought in a lab: people made lighter, officers marked current or not active

- **Nov 19 2026: Project Overview rethought in a lab: people made lighter, officers marked current or not active.**
  **The designer's brief.** With a screenshot of the Published by and Project managers cards: "This is not looking visually well in terms of UX. Can you rethink how the whole page displays the project overview. Is there any such reference available online? Check and report. Especially the Data owner and the project officers must feel less overwhelming. We also want to show if the project officers are current or not active as a status. Think completely differently in the way we present information here. Create this in proto and we will work."
  **Principles at stake (0.10).** Say less (1): the same people are listed in Published by and again in Project managers, each person takes five lines at one size, the hero already names the owners, and "Short title" repeats the page's own heading. One thing first (2): nothing wins, so a person looking for who to contact has no place to start. Reveal in steps (6): the contact first, the rest a line or a click away. Design every state (7): no one named, one, twelve, and a new one, no one is current. Tell the truth (4): status is not in the data, so the lab holds it and says it in words. Where they pull apart: Say less against Make it obvious. A person behind a click is less to read and harder to find. The options sit at different points on that line.
  **What was built (a lab only, `app/proto/project-overview/`; nothing in the product changed).** `people.ts` (placeholder people and real partner organisations, five states, the not-active set held beside the data), `people-ui.tsx` (status badge, contact lines, the directory table, the people dialog, a person on one line, the fold), `overview.tsx` (the three Overviews), `page.tsx` (the lab: the real Overview as it is, then three options; a "People shown" tool for the states). Registered in `app/proto/page.tsx` and `production-routes.ts`. (1) **Directory:** one table of everyone, a line each, status a column, current first, not active last in a quieter colour; the product's embedded table (4.2h). (2) **Contact first:** a card per data owner, its primary contact as the one focal point with email and phone, the other officers one line each with a quiet mail link, the ones who are not active folded under "Show 1 not active" (open at the start where no one is current). (3) **Summary:** the page keeps one line per owner (name, how many, the person to contact) and a "View all" button; the people open in a dialog as the directory table.
  **Changes every option makes to the page, for the designer to confirm one by one (0.4, 4.7).** The "Overview" heading and its line are not drawn (the tab says it, 2.12). "Short title" is not a row (it is the hero's heading, 4.3). A full title shows only where it differs. A project manager is a person whose role is Project Manager (16 Oct 2026), so there is no second Project managers list; the role says it once. Every person has a status, Current or Not active, as a dot badge in words and colour, the same badge the project's own status uses. A person who is not active has no mail link and is quieter. New words: "People" (options 1 and 3), "Other project officers" and "Primary contact" (option 2), "Current", "Not active", "View all", "Show N not active"; none is in the product yet.
  **Decided by the agent, to confirm.** Initials-only avatars were drawn on every row and in the stack, then taken out: they repeat the name and mark every row alike (2.12). The one avatar left is the design system's person on each owner's primary contact. The role that sees no person details (anyone outside DEW, 11 Nov 2026) sees the owners' names in every option and nothing else. Status is held as a set of officer ids in the lab: it is not added to the data model, the registration form or the officer editor.
  **Research (reported to the designer, not a source of styling, 2.6).** GOV.UK's DfE "manage free school projects" design history moved contacts to a tab so the about-the-project page stayed short; Total Synergy's project overview has a People panel and a clickable status; Ghostwriter separates contacts from team members and marks one primary; Primer says an avatar stack is for limited space, two to four avatars, and not where they fit side by side; hover cards are for preview-level information, with a click popover where there are actions, and need a keyboard and touch way in; Atlassian's accessibility notes say a status is read from text, not an indicator alone; Notion pins up to four properties under the title and puts the rest in a side panel; Linear has a summary, a longer description and a details sidebar. Two pages could not be read directly (the DfE contacts page returned 503, Linear's overview 404), so those two rest on search summaries.
  **Open.** (1) Which option, or a mix; the lab can be changed from here. (2) Whether Project managers stays its own card (CONTRACTS 4.7 says project managers have their own card) or becomes the role on a person, as all three options draw it. (3) Where the status is set and what it is called: "Current" and "Not active" are the designer's words; whether it needs a date (since when) or is a switch in the officer's form, and whether the registration asks it (a new officer is current). (4) Whether a person who is not active is hidden by default (option 2) or only sorted last (option 1). (5) "People" as the name of the section. (6) The design system has no avatar group, so a stack would be a new component (1.4); none was kept. (7) The sections under Overview (Data collection, Privacy and restrictions) are not in the options and are unchanged.
  **Verified.** `npx tsc --noEmit` clean; `npx eslint app/proto/project-overview` clean; `npm run check:contracts` OK. In the browser (Playwright, 1440px and 900px, scrollbars drawn, BioData Admin and Public user): all four options render with 0 console errors across five states (two owners, twelve people, one, no one current, none named); the directory has no truncated cell and no sideways scroll at 1440px, the cells of a row share one centre line, and "Not active" is gray-600 on white; the dialog opens from "View all" or an owner row with the keyboard, closes on Escape and returns focus to the button; the fold toggles with Enter and reports `aria-expanded`; the public user sees owners' names and no email, phone or person in all three options. Type measured: Barlow 14px throughout, names 500 (primary, or tertiary when not active), section titles 600, labels 12px 600 uppercase tracked, taken from the Overview card's own treatments. Not verified: touch emulation, roles other than BioData Admin and Public user, a screen reader, the twelve-person table's second page, editing (the lab draws no Edit controls). Not committed.
