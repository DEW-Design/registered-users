# 2026-11-18 - Expanded workspace reuses the form's map layout, and a press on an area selects it

- **Nov 18 2026: Changed in the lab (`app/proto/expand-and-draw/`). Nothing in the product.** The designer, with a screenshot of the expanded workspace, on its map's icon column: "controls look a bit weird, can we just reuse the same layout on the map draw when unexpanded?"; and on the Areas list: "If I've selected an area why can't I edit it after?"
  - **1. One layout, expanded or not.** The map's controls were a different design in the workspace (an icon-only column of two tools at the map's corner, a pill, a card) from the form's (the step header above the map with labelled Polygon and Circle buttons, the way on below it). Now both are one component, `StepMap` (`step-map.tsx`): the header above the map with the step, the line of what to do, the shape's numbers as facts and the step's actions; "go to a place" on the map while choosing; the check bar below the map. In the form it carries the Expand button; in the workspace it does not (it is already expanded) and it takes the height there is. Checked in the workspace: the header reads "STEP 1 OF 3, CHOOSE" with the labelled buttons Polygon and Circle, no icon column, no Expand button.
  - **2. One place for the actions of a shape (removes a duplicate).** The workspace's panel had its own "Use this area", "Redraw", "Done" and "Delete" for the selected shape, and the bar did too. They are now only in the header and bar of `StepMap`; the panel is the numbers (the editable coordinates) and the list. Checked: 0 "Use this area" buttons in the panel.
  - **3. A fourth state of the header: editing.** An area that is already in (selected from the list or the map) was shown under "Step 1, choose", which said nothing about what it was. The header now reads "EDITING", with the shape's facts, "Drag a point, or type exact numbers beside the map." (in the form: "Drag a point to move it. Exact numbers are in Expand."), and Delete and Done.
  - **4. Why the area could not be edited after it was selected (the second note).** Adding a place selects the area and shows its editor; a press on its row in the list then toggled the selection off, so a person pressing the row to select it deselected it and the editor went away (checked before the fix: editor 1, press the row, editor 0, handles 0). A press on a row now selects and does not toggle, and it brings the shape into view (the map went from a 200 km scale to 5 km after zooming out and pressing the row). Pressing the row again keeps it selected; the way out is Done, or pressing the map. While none is selected the list says "Select an area to edit it." Checked: after the press the header says EDITING, the row is highlighted, the coordinates are in the panel, and a radius typed as 30 gave "Radius 30 km, Area 2,827 km²". Done in "Split" too.
  - **5. Two small things.** The workspace's top-right button reads "Use and finish" while a shape is still being checked (the bar's "Use this area" adds it and stays; the header's adds it and closes), so two buttons with one label no longer do two things. The lab's picker pill is at the top on this page, since the workspace has its own bar along the bottom that the pill sat over.
  - **Agent calls (each reversible, in the files named).** "Editing" as the header's fourth step and its two lines; "Use and finish"; the pill at the top; a press on a row selects and zooms.
