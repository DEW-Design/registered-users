# 2026-11-20 - A species search no longer draws a circle over the state

- **Nov 20 2026: Changed in the product (`app/pages/observations/observations-search.tsx`).** The designer, with a screenshot of Explore after typing "s" in the search field (a 1,000 km circle with a pin over South Australia): "another thing the business mentioned was that they do not want the circle to show up by default (for species search), i.e., when I search for a species, a circle is auto-drawn? They find it to be unintuitive to have the circle."
  - **Cause.** A word with no area searches "All of South Australia", which the page models as a real area (a circle of 1,000 km radius round -32, 135, `wholeStateBoundary`) so the one spatial filter has no special case. The map drew it like any area the person had drawn: a big shaded circle and a pin, and the map fitted itself to it.
  - **Now.** The whole-state area still filters (the results, counts and table are as before) but is not given to the map: no circle, no pin, and the map does not move to fit it (it stays at the state-wide view). A search from the header (`?q=`), which uses the same area, behaves the same. An area the person adds (a drawn circle or polygon, coordinates, parks, a shapefile) is drawn and fitted exactly as before; the whole-state area is only ever implied or set by the header search.
  - **Measured (20 Nov 2026, Chromium headless, 1600 by 950).** A search for "kangaroo": no large shape and no pin on the map, 7 dots, Species 7 (the same count as with the circle), no area chip. After drawing a circle: one large shape and one pin, 2 dots, chip "CIRCLE 2", Species 2 (narrowed, as before). `?q=emu` from the header: no circle, no pin, 4 dots, Species 4. Screenshot looked at: the dots sit over the state, nothing else. Zero console errors; `tsc`, `eslint --max-warnings=0` on `observations`, and `check:contracts` clean.
  - **Not checked.** The "Map and table" view with a keyword (the same map layer, not opened); the saved search restored on a revisit; what the map does when a keyword gives results all in one corner (it stays state-wide, it does not zoom to them).
  - **Open.** Whether the map should fit to the results' dots when there is no area (a kangaroo search would then zoom to where the kangaroos are); it did not before and is not done here.
  - **Touches:** contracts 2.12, 4.3.
